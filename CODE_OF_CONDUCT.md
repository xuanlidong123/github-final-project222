# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive environment for all contributors and users. We encourage participation from everyone regardless of background or experience.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior include:

- Showing respect and consideration toward others.
- Communicating clearly and professionally.
- Accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment or discrimination.
- Personal attacks or insulting comments.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any behavior that creates an unsafe environment.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of acceptable behavior. They may take appropriate actions in response to unacceptable behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including repositories, discussions, issues, pull requests, and other community interactions.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to project maintainers. All reports will be reviewed and investigated appropriately.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers may take actions including:

- Removing inappropriate comments or contributions.
- Issuing warnings.
- Temporarily restricting participation.
- Permanently banning individuals from project activities.
